Common questions

Before you ask — maybe we already answered it.

Is WorkGrid really free?

Yes, during early access. Full features, no limits, no credit card. When pricing launches, early users get locked-in discounts. We'll notify you well before any changes.

How fast do you respond?

Email: within 12 hours. Live chat: within minutes during active hours. Twitter/DM: within a few hours. We're a solo project, but we take support seriously.

Can I export my data?

Yes. You can export tasks, contacts, deals, and invoices as CSV anytime. Your data is yours — we never lock it in. If you decide WorkGrid isn't for you, take everything with you.

What happens when pricing launches?

Early users keep their accounts at a locked-in rate — significantly lower than public pricing. You'll be notified 30 days before any change with the option to continue, downgrade, or export your data.

Is my data secure?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Role-based access means HR data is invisible to non-HR roles at every layer — UI, API, and database. We don't sell or share your data. Ever.

Can I request a feature?

Absolutely — and we actually listen. Check our Roadmap page to vote on features. Popular requests get built faster. This isn't a suggestion black hole — votes directly influence our sprint planning.

Do you offer refunds?

Since WorkGrid is currently free, this doesn't apply yet. When pricing launches, we'll offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on paid plans. No questions asked.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account → Delete. Export your data first, then confirm deletion. Everything is wiped permanently within 24 hours. No retention, no "we'll keep it just in case."

Prefer to reach out directly?

No forms, no filters. Just direct access.
