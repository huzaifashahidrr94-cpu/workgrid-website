For Agencies

Multiple clients. Multiple teams. One source of truth.

Running an agency means juggling projects, clients, invoices, and team capacity — often across the same workday. WorkGrid keeps it all visible without the chaos.

The agency pain points

Client work bleeds together

Tasks for Client A sit next to tasks for Client B. One missed label and someone works on the wrong project.

Invoicing is manual and slow

Someone on your team spends hours each month creating invoices, tracking payments, and following up on overdue ones.

No visibility into team capacity

Who's overloaded? Who's idle? You can't tell because task tools don't show workload across projects.

How WorkGrid solves it

Per-client workspaces

Organize projects by client. Each client's tasks, deals, and invoices are separate. No cross-contamination.

Batch invoicing

Generate invoices for multiple clients in minutes, not hours. Payment status auto-tracks. Overdue reminders are automatic.

Team workload view

See who's working on what, who's at capacity, and who can take more. Assign work based on reality, not guessing.

A day in your agency's WorkGrid life

1

Morning standup — visually

Dashboard shows all active projects, team workload, and overdue items. No 30-minute meeting needed.

2

Assign and track

Move tasks across the board. Assign team members. Set priorities. Everyone sees their queue instantly.

3

Invoice at project milestones

Phase 1 done? Generate an invoice. Link it to the project. Client gets it, you track it. No manual copying.

4

Review pipeline and capacity

See incoming deals, team availability, and revenue forecasts. Decide if you can take that new client.

For Remote Teams

Aligned across time zones. No 9 AM standups required.

Remote work fails when nobody knows what's happening. WorkGrid gives your team a shared source of truth that works 24/7 — even when half the team is asleep.

The remote team struggles

Invisible work

"What's the status on that?" is asked 20 times a day because nobody can see what anyone else is working on.

Time zone deadlocks

Someone in London is blocked waiting for a response from someone in San Francisco who won't be online for 8 hours.

Disconnected tools

Slack for chat. Notion for docs. Trello for tasks. Loom for updates. Four tools, four places to check, four things to forget.

How WorkGrid keeps remote teams connected

Work is visible by default

Kanban boards, task assignees, and deal stages are visible to everyone. No "what are you working on?" messages needed.

Async-first workflows

Update tasks, move deals, leave comments — all without needing someone online right now. Work flows 24/7.

Smart notifications

Get notified about what matters — tasks assigned to you, deal updates, overdue invoices — not every single change in the workspace.

Your remote team's WorkGrid day

1

9 AM in your time zone (whatever that is)

Log in. Dashboard shows what happened while you slept — tasks completed, deals moved, comments added. You're caught up in 2 minutes.

2

Work without waiting

Move your tasks, add comments, update deal stages. Your teammates will see it when they come online. No sync delays.

3

Hand off cleanly

4

End of day — log off

Your board is updated. Your tasks are tracked. Your deals are moving. Tomorrow, you pick up right where you left off. No status report needed.
