The Story

I built WorkGrid because I was tired of suffering

I've used HubSpot. I've used Salesforce. I've tried Monday, ClickUp, Notion-as-a-CRM, and about a dozen others. Every single time, the same story: too complex, too expensive, or missing something critical.

I'd end up running tasks in one app, deals in another, invoices in a third, and HR stuff in Google Sheets. That's not a workspace — that's a mess.

So I built what I actually wanted: one tool that handles tasks, clients, deals, invoicing, and HR — with role-based privacy so everyone sees only what they should. Simple to start, powerful enough to scale.
